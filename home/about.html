Crafting Brilliance Since 2019

Redefining Luxury & Precision

Founded in 2019, Lyxen began its journey as a dedicated venture in the diamond and jewellery manufacturing sector. What started as a passionate offline craftsmanship enterprise has now grown into a trusted powerhouse of certified diamond sourcing and bespoke fine jewellery creation.

In 2022, to bridge the gap between traditional wholesale trade and global digital accessibility, we officially launched our comprehensive e-commerce and inventory portal. This strategic expansion allowed us to serve international clients, retail partners, and jewellery connoisseurs worldwide with absolute transparency and ease.

Certified Diamond Jewellery & Purity Standards

At Lyxen, every single piece of diamond jewellery and loose diamond is crafted and verified under stringent international parameters. We ensure that our clients receive 100% authentic, ethical, and top-grade certified items.

✓
GIA & IGI Certified Diamonds
All our diamond jewelry and loose stones come with world-renowned GIA (Gemological Institute of America) and IGI certificates, guaranteeing exact grading on Cut, Color, Clarity, and Carat (4Cs).
✓
100% Personal & Laser Inscribed
Every diamond features a unique laser-inscribed tracking number on its girdle matching its official certificate, ensuring complete authenticity and personal peace of mind.
✓
Ethical & Conflict-Free Sourcing
We strictly adhere to the Kimberley Process, ensuring all our diamonds are 100% natural, conflict-free, and ethically sourced from responsible mines.
✓
Master Craftsmanship & Hallmarking
Our master artisans craft fine settings in gold and platinum, accompanied by official purity hallmarks and rigorous multi-stage quality inspections.
